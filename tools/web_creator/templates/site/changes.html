What's changed

Familiar bones,
new everything else.

A quick tour of the systems we've reworked. None of this is final — alpha means everything is on the table.

01 · Combat

Damage types matter again

Slashing, piercing, blunt and hand-to-hand resistances are now meaningful and visible in encounter design. The same goes for elements — every spell school has a place because every enemy has something to exploit.

Wikis will be wrong. Bring the right weapon, the right element, the right strategy — and groups with different jobs will solve the same fight in different ways.

02 · Itemisation

An ARPG-lite gear loop

Thousands of existing items have had their stats redistributed, and brand new pieces have been added to fill out the levelling path. Treasure Caskets dropped from kills are the main source of new gear, with rare Glowing Chests for the truly lucky.

Random augments mean you'll always have something new to try on — but the iconic pieces (Haubergeon, Scorpion Harness, abjurations) remain untouched and worth chasing.

03 · Treasure Hunter

No more THF tax

Treasure Hunter is no longer tied to a single job. Drop-rate bonuses are now Key Items earned through story and content progression — they reward longevity and spread naturally across the playerbase.

Thief gets a new identity in return: Mug now lowers enemy defence, Gilfinder is buffed, and a new Casket Hunter trait increases casket drops and quality.

04 · Job balance

Every job has a seat

Every base job is being tuned around the new combat. Some jobs lose their old "must-bring" identity (sorry, THF), but gain new ones — Warrior leans into damage-type vulnerability stacking, Monk's Footwork has rhythm, Thief's Feint cracks open enemy defences for the whole party.

Patch notes preview

A taste of the balance work

Real entries from the current alpha balance pass — to give you a feel for how granular the rework gets.

Warrior · Aggressor

Stacks a damage-type vulnerability on hit

Each successful melee hit while Aggressor is active applies +5% damage taken of the Warrior's equipped weapon type, stacking up to 3 times for +15%. Stacks reset on target switch.

Warrior · Tomahawk

Reworked into a flat 20% physical opener

Previously did nothing on neutral-resistance enemies. Now applies a flat 20% vulnerability for the equipped physical damage type, duration scaled by merits.

Monk · Footwork

Every third kick crits

Footwork now has a rhythm: every third successful kick lands as a guaranteed critical, then the counter resets.

Monk · Focus

Crits dismantle enemy DEF

Critical hits during Focus now stack −20 DEF on the target, up to 3 stacks. Cleared on target switch or expiry.

Thief · Feint

A party-wide physical opener

EVA down reduced from 150 → 75. Now also applies a 5% physical vulnerability across all four physical types for 60s. Stacks with Warrior's Tomahawk.

Thief · Sneak Attack & Trick Attack

Pickpocket: every strike takes something

Sneak Attack drains 10 DEX for 30s. Trick Attack drains 10 AGI for 30s. Reduces target evasion and accuracy.
