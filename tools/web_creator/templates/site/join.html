Step into the alpha.

No application, no whitelist. Drop into Discord, follow the install steps, and we'll see you in Vana'diel.

How to get in

1

Join the Discord

Patch notes, install help, group-finding, and direct line to the dev team all live there.

Join Discord →
2

Have a working FFXI install

You'll need a legitimate, fully-updated retail Final Fantasy XI install on your machine before connecting.

3

Grab the launcher

The current alpha uses a custom launcher available in the #install channel of our Discord. Pinned messages have the latest build.

4

Make a character & say hi

Servers register on launch — no extra account creation needed. Drop a "hello" in #general; the existing players are good about pointing newcomers at the early game.

At a glance

What to expect

  • Cap: Level 75 (Wings of the Goddess era)
  • Trusts: Up to 3
  • XP: 1.0× · Craft: 1.5×
  • Conveniences: Survival Guides, Home Points, no JP Midnight
  • Population: Small, friendly, alpha
  • Cost: Free. Non-commercial.

⚠ Heads up — this is alpha

Patches arrive often. Abilities may behave differently between sessions. Save your weird bugs and bring them to #bug-reports.
