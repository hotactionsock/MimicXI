Vision

Familiar, yet unknown.

MimicXI is a fresh experience that re-imagines content rather than recreating it. We're keeping the heart of FFXI — the jobs, the world, the rhythm — but rebuilding combat, gear, and progression so that wikis won't tell you the answers anymore. The discoveries are yours to make again.

Combat with consequence

Damage types and elemental weaknesses actually matter. Bring the right weapon, exploit the right element — homogenised "always-best" strategies are gone.

An ARPG-lite gear loop

Thousands of items reworked. Treasure caskets and glowing chests drop randomised augments, so every level brings something new to try on.

Treasure for everyone

Treasure Hunter is no longer locked behind a single job. Drop bonuses are tied to story progression — a key item the whole group benefits from.

What sets MimicXI apart

Built for the way you actually play.

Solo or in a group

Trusts are enabled (up to 3) so you can play on your schedule, but the world is tuned to reward grouping when challenges scale up.

Casual hours, real progress

Streamlined missions, no JP Midnight, Survival Guides and Home Points active. We respect your evening.

Every job belongs

From Blue Mage to Dancer, every job is being shaped to find a useful seat at the table — not just the meta picks.

Discovery first

Wikis won't help much here. New camps, new NM behaviours, new drops — the joy is in finding out.
