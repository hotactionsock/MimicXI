Rules & conduct

Be kind. Have fun.

A short list — not a wall. The spirit matters more than the letter. Staff will use judgement.

01

Be respectful

Treat every player like someone you'd want at your table. No harassment, slurs, or targeted unkindness — in-game, in Discord, or anywhere our community gathers.

02

No real-money trading

This is a free, non-commercial server. Selling gil, items, accounts or services for real money is not allowed under any circumstances.

03

No botting or unattended automation

Macros are fine; bots are not. If your character is gathering, fighting, or earning while you're asleep, it's a bot. Trusts are not a loophole — they're a feature.

04

One household, two boxes

Two-boxing is supported and encouraged. More than that on the same account or household, please ask staff first.

05

Report exploits, don't farm them

Alpha will have bugs. Some will be funny, some will be powerful. If you find one that obviously breaks the economy or trivialises content, tell us — don't quietly farm it. We'd rather thank you than roll back your gear.

06

Keep it to the game

Don't use the server, our Discord, or our channels to advertise other communities, sell things, or post NSFW content.

07

Feedback is a gift

We read every patch reaction. We won't make snap changes from one bad night, but we promise to listen, and to explain when we choose differently.

Questions or appeals

If something feels off — a ruling, an interaction, a bug — bring it to staff in Discord. We'd rather hear it than not.
